Meet the founder

Landon Jones.

“We are going to create spectacular and engaging content together. Let's turn your vision into reality.”

A lifelong Floridian based in Key West, Landon started Digital Peak in 2016. His first major client, The Standard in Gainesville, hired him before construction began. Since then he's spent two years producing alongside the Florida Keys TDC, shot 20+ resorts for Marriott, and is lead cinematographer on the documentary Shrimpboat Sounds, coming in 2027.

He also brings students onto shoots as shooters, because the next generation of Florida filmmakers should learn on real sets.

So what do we do?

Social strategy & video production.

With a master's in Mass Communications and Social Media, Landon brings a dual threat to the table: the production chops to make it beautiful and the strategy to make it work.

We produce everything from retail advertising to in-motion, high-speed shoots, specializing in television commercials, videography and photography for content marketing, and high-intensity outdoor work. Hearing your story is the first step toward creating something incredible.
