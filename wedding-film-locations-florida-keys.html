Best Wedding Film Locations in the Florida Keys

June 6, 20252 min readBy Landon Jones

Congrats! The Florida Keys are your wedding backdrop. Now, let's capture the magic on film. But where to begin? The Keys offer scenic locations galore.

At Digital Peak Productions, wedding videography is an adventure. We call it a "content safari." We find settings reflecting your love story. So, join us! We'll unveil the best wedding film locations. Discover top venues and local vendors. Make your Florida Keys wedding film unforgettable.

Content Safari: 5 Film Locations:

  • Key West Lighthouse:
  • Why? It boasts dramatic architecture and ocean views. History is also present. Imagine romance with the lighthouse as a backdrop. Especially at golden hour.
  • Filming Tip: Climb to the top. This symbolizes your journey. Capture breathtaking footage.
  • Truman Little White House:
  • Why? Presidential elegance and charm await. Manicured gardens add beauty. Classic architecture sets the atmosphere.
  • Filming Tip: Use the porch for interviews. Capture Key West's laid-back vibe.
  • Southernmost Point Buoy:
  • Why? It's a Key West icon! This quirky spot is perfect for fun shots.
  • Filming Tip: Embrace the crowds. Capture the energy. Get creative with angles.
  • Bahia Honda State Park Beaches:
  • Why? White sand and turquoise waters sparkle. Palm trees sway gently. This is tropical paradise.
  • Filming Tip: Capture the sunset. It's romantic and dramatic. Use drones to showcase the coast.
  • African Queen Canal Cruise (Key Largo):
  • Why? Experience a unique nautical adventure. Enjoy the charm of a historic boat. A truly memorable location.
  • Filming Tip: Capture the boat gliding. Mangrove forests create a backdrop. Perfect for intimate moments.

Top Wedding Venues in the Florida Keys:

  • Ocean Key Resort & Spa (Key West): Beachfront setting with sunset views.
  • Casa Marina (Key West): Historic elegance with grand ballrooms.
  • Moorings Village (Islamorada): Secluded and romantic with a private beach.
  • Pierre's Restaurant & Morada Bay Beach Cafe (Islamorada): Chic with a bohemian vibe.
  • Caribbean Resort (Islamorada): Waterfront views and luxurious accommodations.

Local Vendor Recommendations:

  • DJ/Entertainment:

Taste the Keys: Local Cuisine:

Showcase Keys flavors at your reception. Support local businesses and delight your guests.

  • Fresh Seafood: Try conch fritters and Key West pink shrimp.
  • Key Lime Pie: This is the quintessential dessert!
  • Cuban Influences: Ropa vieja is another tasty option.
  • Local Brews: Pair your meal with Florida Keys Brewing Co. craft beers.
