Why Wedding Videography is a Must-Have in the Florida Keys:

  • Relive the Magic: Photos capture moments, but videos capture emotions. Hear your vows again, see the laughter on your loved ones' faces, and relive the energy of your reception.
  • Share with Loved Ones: Can't invite everyone to your destination wedding? A wedding video allows you to share your special day with friends and family who couldn't be there.
  • The Keys' Unique Beauty: The Florida Keys offer a distinct visual landscape. A skilled videographer knows how to utilize the stunning scenery, the golden light, and the vibrant colors to create a truly breathtaking film.

Tips and Tricks for Hiring a Wedding Videographer in the Florida Keys:

  • Start Your Search Early: Popular videographers book up quickly, especially during peak wedding season. Begin your search as soon as you've set your date and venue.
  • Review Portfolios and Demo Reels: Don't just look at the highlight reels! Ask to see full wedding videos to get a better sense of the videographer's style and quality.
  • Read Reviews and Testimonials: See what other couples have to say about their experience working with the videographer.
  • Consider Your Style: Do you prefer a cinematic, documentary, or traditional style? Make sure the videographer's style aligns with your vision.
  • Understand Packages and Pricing: Get a clear breakdown of what's included in each package, including the number of hours of coverage, the type of equipment used, and the final deliverables.

Essential Interview Questions to Ask Your Potential Videographer:

  • Experience and Expertise:
  • How many weddings have you filmed in the Florida Keys?
  • What's your experience filming at our chosen venue?
  • What type of equipment do you use? Do you have backup equipment?
  • Style and Approach:
  • Can you describe your videography style?
  • How do you approach filming a wedding?
  • How do you handle unexpected situations or challenges on the wedding day?
  • Can you share your Instagram or favorite content?
  • Logistics and Deliverables:
  • What's included in your different packages?
  • How long will it take to receive the final video?
  • What format will the video be in?
  • Do you offer raw footage?
  • What is your payment schedule?
  • Do you have liability insurance?
  • Personality and Connection:
  • This is crucial! Do you feel comfortable with them? Do they understand your vision? Do you trust them to capture your special day?

A great question to ask is if they are on Wedding Wire or The Knot. And, why so? Some professionals love the network and others stray away.
