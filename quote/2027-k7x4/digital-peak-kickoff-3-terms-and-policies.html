Terms & policies

The fine print,
in plain English.

No surprises. This is how booking, payment, changes and ownership work at Digital Peak. Your signed production agreement is the official version. This page explains it.

01 Booking & payment

  • Deposit: A 50% deposit and a signed agreement lock your dates. We can't hold dates without both.
  • Balance: The remaining 50% is due on final delivery, net 15. For projects over $25,000, we bill 50% to book, 25% after the shoot and 25% on delivery.
  • Expenses: Travel, permits, talent, stock and specialty rentals are listed in your estimate and billed at cost (rentals at cost + 15%).
  • How to pay: ACH or check is preferred. Cards are accepted with a 3% processing fee.
  • Late payment: Balances more than 30 days past due accrue 1.5% per month. Final files are released once the balance is paid in full. You'll get watermarked review copies before that.

02 Cancellation & rescheduling

Once your dates are booked, we turn other work away and start paying crew, gear and pre-production costs. These tiers cover that commitment. Each percentage is a share of the production-day fees in your estimate.

Pre-production work already completed and any non-refundable third-party costs (travel, talent, rentals, permits) are billed in every case. If Digital Peak has to cancel for reasons within our control, we'll reschedule at no cost or refund what you've paid for work not yet done.

Weather

Florida weather does its own thing. We watch the forecast with you and make the call together 24–48 hours ahead. A weather reschedule called at least 24 hours out costs nothing. If we call weather after crew call on shoot day, the crew is billed at 50% of their day rates. Drone flights may be grounded for wind, rain or airspace activity on any day. That isn't a cancellation, and we'll shoot ground coverage instead.

03 Hours, revisions & changes

  • Shoot days: 10 hours including lunch. Overtime is 1.5× for hours 10–12 and 2× after that, and it's always approved by your on-set lead first.
  • Revisions: Two rounds are included with every edit. Each round is one consolidated set of notes. Additional rounds are $125/hr.
  • Change orders: New scenes, locations, deliverables, reshoots or a new creative direction after the treatment is approved fall outside the original scope. We'll quote it and get your written OK before doing the work.
  • Feedback windows: If a review sits longer than 15 business days, we may need to reschedule your project in our post queue.

04 Ownership & usage

  • Your final deliverables: Once paid in full, you get a perpetual, worldwide license to use the final videos and photos to market your business, in any media.
  • Raw footage & project files: These stay with Digital Peak and aren't included by default. Raw footage buyouts are available on request.
  • Music & stock: Licensed for the uses named in your brief. Moving into paid ads or broadcast later may need a license upgrade, and we'll handle it.
  • Talent: Usage terms follow each talent agreement. Paid usage or long terms can carry extra fees.
  • Our portfolio: We may share the finished work on our website, reel, social and award entries after it's public, unless we've agreed in writing to keep it confidential.

05 Delivery, archive & insurance

  • Delivery: Files arrive by download link, active for 30 days. Please download and back them up.
  • Archive: We keep your project for 12 months after delivery. After that, we can't guarantee files can be recovered. Extended archiving is available.
  • Insurance: We carry general liability, equipment and drone coverage. Certificates of insurance are available on request, and we can add your property as additionally insured.
  • Safety: Everyone's safety comes before any shot. Our crew can pause or adjust a setup or a flight if conditions aren't safe.
This summary goes with your production agreement and doesn't replace it. If they ever differ, the signed agreement wins.
