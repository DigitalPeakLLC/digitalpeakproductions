This is the roadmap for your project. It covers what happens at each stage, what we'll need from you, and where we'll ask for your sign-off. Once everyone knows the plan, the shoot goes smoothly and the edit lands where it should.

Larger projects, multiple locations and talent casting can add time. Social-first projects often move faster. We'll give you a dated schedule at kickoff.

01
Week 1

Discovery & kickoff

We get clear on what you're making, who it's for, and what success looks like.

What we do
  • Run the kickoff meeting (that's this one)
  • Turn your goals into a written scope and estimate
  • Send the production agreement and deposit invoice
What we need from you
  • Your completed Creative Brief (see Before We Roll)
  • One decision-maker and one day-to-day contact
  • Brand guidelines, logos and any references you love
Sign-off: Signed agreement + 50% deposit. This locks your shoot dates on our calendar.
02
Weeks 1–3

Pre-production

This is where most of the quality comes from. Good prep makes for an easy shoot day.

What we do
  • Creative treatment: concept, tone, music direction
  • Shot list and shoot-day schedule
  • Location scout (virtual or in person), permits, drone airspace
  • Book crew, talent, stylists and any specialty gear
  • Final call sheet 48 hours before the shoot
What we need from you
  • Feedback on the treatment within 3 business days
  • Location access, contacts and any restricted areas
  • Talent/employee availability and signed releases
  • Product, F&B or rooms prepped per the shot list
Sign-off: Treatment + shot list approval. After this point, new scenes or locations become a change order.
03
Shoot day

Production

We bring the crew, the gear and the plan. You bring the access and a quick yes or no when we need one.

What we do
  • Arrive, load in and set up ahead of the first shot
  • Run the schedule and capture every shot on the list, plus the happy accidents
  • Back up all footage on set, twice
  • Keep you updated on progress during the day
What we need from you
  • One on-set point person with the authority to make calls
  • Access to locations at the agreed times
  • Flexibility for weather, light and guests. We'll adapt with you
04
Weeks 3–5

Post-production

We build the story in the edit, then refine it with you over two included rounds of revisions. Picture lock comes before color and sound finishing.

What we do
  • First cut in about 7–10 business days
  • Private review link with timecoded comments
  • Fine cut with your round-one notes applied
  • Color grade, sound mix, music, graphics and captions
What we need from you
  • One consolidated set of notes per round
  • Feedback within 3–5 business days of each cut
  • Any legal, brand or stakeholder review scheduled in advance
05
Week 5–6

Delivery & archive

You get final files ready for every platform you named in the brief.

What we do
  • Final masters plus web, social and broadcast versions as scoped
  • Photography selects in full-res and web sizes
  • A download link that stays active for 30 days
  • Project archived for 12 months
What we need from you
  • Final balance paid. Files are released on payment
  • Download and back up your files within 30 days
  • Tag us when it goes live. We love to share it

— How to give great feedback

  • Consolidate. Gather everyone's notes into one list per round. Conflicting notes from different stakeholders are the #1 cause of delays.
  • Use timecodes. "At 0:42, swap the pool shot" beats "the middle part feels off."
  • Separate musts from maybes. Mark which notes are required and which are preferences.
  • Tell us the why. "This feels too corporate" helps us more than "change the song."
  • Bring approvers in early. If your GM, brand team or legal will review, they should see the first cut, not the final.
  • Know the difference. A revision changes what we shot. A new scene, location or deliverable is a change in scope.

— How we'll communicate

  • You'll have one point of contact at Digital Peak from kickoff to delivery.
  • We reply within one business day, and faster during shoot week.
  • Key decisions and approvals are confirmed in writing (email is fine) so nothing gets lost.
