03 Good to know

  • A day is 10 hours, including a meal break. A half day is up to 5 hours, billed at 60% of the day rate.
  • Overtime is 1.5× from hours 10–12 and 2× after that.
  • Travel days are billed at 50% of the day rate. Flights, lodging and mileage outside the Keys are billed at cost.
  • Weekly gear holds are billed at 4× the day rate for 7 days.
  • Specialty items such as big HMIs, an Easyrig or cinema zooms are sourced from our rental partners at cost + 15%.
  • Dry-hire gear rental (no Digital Peak crew) requires approval, a COI and a security deposit.
  • Projects book with a 50% deposit. Full terms are in our Terms & Policies.
  • Rates are valid through December 31, 2027. Applicable taxes are additional.
