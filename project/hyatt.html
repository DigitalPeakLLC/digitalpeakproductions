Hyatt Centric South Beach · the film with Isaac Maiselman ↗

Hyatt Centric sells a neighborhood as much as a hotel. For their South Beach property we blended Hyatt's brand tone with Miami Beach energy: rooftop, rooms, dining and the street outside.

The brief

Show the hotel as the best way to experience South Beach, for leisure guests and for the corporate groups the property wanted more of.

The approach

Directed by Isaac Maiselman and shot by Digital Peak, the project followed our standard 4–6 week flow: pre-planning, a tight production window and two to three weeks of post.

We built a library of hero, room and lifestyle content cut for the website, paid social and the sales team.

The result

The new content drove a significant increase in engagement and a boost in corporate clientele, and it became the template for the hospitality work that followed.
