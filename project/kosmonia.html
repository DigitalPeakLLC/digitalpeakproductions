Kosmonia: Reflections that sell · the film

Kosmonia makes luxury mirrors. They asked us to turn a reflection into a story, and to make it work for social audiences from New York to Shanghai.

The brief

Redefine how luxury mirrors are sold online: elegant, aspirational and built for the scroll, with versions ready for international markets.

The work

We built a combined brand ad plus talent-led spots like “Conquer with Jess” and “Madi's Morning Glow,” each showing the mirror as part of a daily ritual, not just a product on a wall.

The result

Careful planning and a tight shoot meant the project finished ahead of schedule and under budget, with a library of social ads ready to run worldwide.
