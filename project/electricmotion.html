Electric Motion: launching the Escape · the film

Electric Motion was bringing the Escape to the American market and needed launch content that made the build quality obvious in a two-second scroll.

In the studio

We built the base layer in the studio with sliders, macro and probe lenses, getting in close enough to show off the finish and engineering of the bike.

Sound and story

A final layer of sound design and voiceover brought the piece to life.

Built to be repurposed

The hero spot was repurposed into more than ten pieces of micro-content, and it became a standout performer on Amazon and other online retailers.
