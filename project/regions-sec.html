Regions Extra: Welcome to the SEC · the film

Regions Extra: Welcome to the SEC introduced the conference's new head coaches to fans, and it premiered on ESPN. Digital Peak shot and edited the entire series.

The series

Each episode sits down with a new SEC head coach on his home turf, hosted by Peter Burns and Chris Doering. The Florida Gators episode with Jon Sumrall, featured here, is a good place to start.

One crew, every campus

We traveled as one team with the whole kit: cinema cameras, lighting, audio and drones. Fly in, build the interview set on campus, capture the coach and the place, pack out and hit the next stop.

Same crew at every stop means every episode looks and sounds like the same show, no matter whose stadium we were standing in.

Tight timelines

Network dates don't move. We turned each shoot into a finished, broadcast-ready episode on a compressed schedule, so the series hit ESPN on time, every time.
