WeGym brand relaunch · the film

WeGym was relaunching its brand and needed content that looked as sharp as the new identity. One day, one studio in Connecticut: four performers, four scenes, four products.

The shoot

We choreographed four scenes that show off the range of WeGym's products, with lighting designed to mirror the brand's look so every frame felt on-identity.

The edit

In post, color grading and clean transitions turned the day into a polished package built for engagement across digital platforms.

The result

Five web-ready videos and more photos than the team could post, all from a single shoot day on a tight schedule.
