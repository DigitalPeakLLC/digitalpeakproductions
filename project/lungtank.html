LungTank in Key West · the film

LungTank, the Australian underwater breathing-tank brand, came to Key West for social ads that show what the product feels like in the water.

Below the surface

Landon and George worked the reefs with underwater housings and GoPros, chasing the color and detail of Key West's marine life while keeping the product the hero of every frame.

Above it

Drones added scale and context, so the ads move between the open water and an intimate underwater point of view.

The cut

In post we edited, graded and tailored each ad to its platform, built to spark curiosity and drive action.
