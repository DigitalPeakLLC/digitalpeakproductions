EcoFlow Trail Series launch · the film

When EcoFlow asked us to lead the launch of the Trail Series, it was never going to be a studio shoot. The product promises portable power that goes anywhere, so we went there too.

The mission

Direct and film a complete launch package: a hero video, three industry-specific cuts, social micro-content and a full photography suite, all proving the product in real, unforgiving terrain.

Pre-production: earning the view

Authenticity was non-negotiable. Our scout covered more than six miles of rugged North Carolina wilderness to find terrain as tough as the product and logistics we could solve on camera.

Production: two days of grit

A 20+ person crew hiked cinema cameras, lighting and EcoFlow units deep into the woods. When heavy rain threatened the schedule, we protected the gear, kept rolling and used the weather for texture.

The talent delivered believable performances through all of it, which is what makes the lifestyle story land.

One shoot, many narratives

The anthem film introduced the Trail Series to the world. Three demographic-specific setups ran in parallel, and a vertical library and high-res stills now fuel EcoFlow's social and web for months.
