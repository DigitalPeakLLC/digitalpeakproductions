We use an assortment of data-receiving platforms to help us better connect with our potential clients.

This Privacy Policy describes how Digital Peak Productions ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information collected through our Google Ads campaigns and related landing pages. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information from users who interact with our Google Ads campaigns and landing pages:

Personal Information: This may include your name, email address, phone number, company name, and any other information you voluntarily submit through forms on our landing pages.

Usage Data: We automatically collect certain information about your visit to our landing pages, such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages visited, and the dates/times of your visit. This data is collected using cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies.

Conversion Data: Google Ads provides us with data about the actions users take on our website after clicking on our ads, such as form submissions, phone calls, and page views. This data is used to measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns.

How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect for the following purposes:

To Respond to Inquiries: To respond to your requests for information about our products or services.

To Provide Services: To provide you with the products or services you have requested.

To Improve Our Website: To analyze usage data and user behavior to improve the design and functionality of our website and landing pages.

To Personalize Your Experience: To personalize your experience on our website and landing pages by providing content and offers tailored to your interests.

To Send Marketing Communications: With your consent, to send you marketing communications about our products, services, and special offers. You may opt-out of receiving these communications at any time by following the instructions in the communication.

To Measure Ad Effectiveness: To analyze conversion data to measure the effectiveness of our Google Ads campaigns and optimize our advertising strategies.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information with the following categories of third parties:

Service Providers: We may share your information with third-party service providers who assist us with website hosting, data analytics, email marketing, and other business operations. These service providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and use it only for the purposes we specify.

Google: We share conversion data with Google for the purpose of measuring the effectiveness of our Google Ads campaigns and optimizing our advertising strategies. This data is subject to Google's privacy policy.

Legal Compliance: We may disclose your information to law enforcement agencies, government authorities, or other third parties if we are required to do so by law or legal process.

Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or other business transfer, your information may be transferred to the acquiring entity.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to collect usage data and personalize your experience on our website and landing pages. Cookies are small data files that are stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Web beacons are small graphic images embedded in web pages or emails that allow us to track user activity.

You can control the use of cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may limit your ability to use certain features of our website.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. These measures include data encryption, firewalls, and secure server configurations. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information.

Data Retention

We will retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Your Rights

You have the following rights with respect to your personal information:

Access: You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you.

Correction: You have the right to request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal information we hold about you.

Deletion: You have the right to request that we delete your personal information, subject to certain exceptions.

Objection: You have the right to object to the processing of your personal information for direct marketing purposes.To exercise these rights, please contact us using the contact information provided below.

Third-Party Links

Our website and landing pages may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these websites before providing them with your personal information.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business practices or legal requirements. We will post any changes to this Privacy Policy on our website.
